## Why
I am not only an IT professional in my work, but also in my free time. I have been fascinated by computers and technology from a young age.

Over the years, I have developed several hobby projects. I have learned a lot from other people's blogs and websites, so I enjoy sharing my own projects to help and inspire others.

## Website
I have many hobbies and interests, and designing a website is one of them. Unfortunately, this takes a lot of time and requires the right skills. Today, AI is a valuable solution for that.

That is why I used Google Stitch to generate a design for this website. The first version after a single prompt is never perfect, but it was a good starting point. I was surprised by how well it worked, partly because it chose an interface that resembles older design tools such as Figma and Adobe XD. That worked well for me because I am used to working with these tools.

After I was satisfied with the design, I set up a new Nuxt project. Nuxt is a framework I work with extensively in my job. I also used Nuxt to build frontends with AI. This allows me to use AI as a tool rather than a replacement. Understanding and checking AI output is incredibly important, and Nuxt helps me do that.

I downloaded the design from Google Stitch and placed it in a folder in the project. I still tried the prompt "build this website," but it turned into a complete mess. Splitting the work into components and pages worked much better. That is how I built the website: starting with the navigation, then the footer, and then the pages.

Because the website is available in Dutch and English, I also used the i18n module. This allows me to offer the website in multiple languages.

I also added a few easter eggs. Have you found them yet? With that, the website was essentially ready in a few hours.

## Conclusion
The website reflects my hobbies and interests, and using AI significantly accelerated the process. It is a fun way to share my projects and inspire others, while continuing to develop my skills in web development and AI applications.
